About Us

Carrying the best of India's farms to the world

Sarvatrik Impex began with a simple belief: the richness of Indian soil deserves to travel the world with care. From our base in Pune, we bring together onion powder, drumstick powder, a growing range of powdered materials and agricultural products, and deliver them to buyers who value quality, consistency and honest dealing.

Who we are

We are a Pune-based import-export company with deep experience in both the export business and in business management. Behind every order stands a team of experienced professionals from a variety of fields, who help us run our business efficiently and effectively, so that every partner receives reliable service, clear communication and products they can count on.

What we stand for

Our mission is to be a dependable bridge between Indian agriculture and global markets, building long-term partnerships grounded in trust, transparency and respect for the land our products come from.
